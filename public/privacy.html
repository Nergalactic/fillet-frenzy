Fillet Frenzy Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026 · Fillet Frenzy is made by Nergalactic.

This policy covers the Fillet Frenzy game for Android (app ID com.nergalactic.filletfrenzy) and on the web. Fillet Frenzy has no accounts or sign-in, and I don't run any servers that collect information about you.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, stars, shop choices, settings and the save codes you create are stored only on your device. They are never sent to me. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Ads (Android app)

The Android app offers optional rewarded video ads, provided by Google AdMob. An ad only plays when you choose to watch one for a bonus. To show ads and measure them, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and approximate location, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the UK, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

Purchases (Android app)

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. I never see your payment details. Google Play tells the app which items your Google account owns so they can be restored on a new device. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads on the web

When you play Fillet Frenzy on a games website, that website and its ad partners (such as GameDistribution) may show ads and collect information under their own privacy policies.

Children

Fillet Frenzy is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer email address shown on the Fillet Frenzy page in the Google Play Store.
